# Privacy Policy for King's Game

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026

Thank you for playing **King's Game** ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how King's Game handles your information when you use our iOS application.

---

### 1. Summary (Privacy by Design)
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
- **Local On-Device Storage Only:** Player names, game settings, language preferences, and gameplay states are stored strictly on your local device.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking:** We do not include third-party tracking SDKs or cross-site tracking tools.
- **Secure Purchases:** All in-app purchases are handled directly and securely by Apple through the App Store.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
We do not collect any personal data or device identifiers, including but not limited to:
- Name, physical address, email address, or phone number
- Precise or coarse location data
- Device contacts, calendar, camera, or microphone data
- Browsing history, search history, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)

---

### 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores minimal operational data exclusively on your device using standard iOS sandboxed local storage (`UserDefaults`):
- **Game Setup & Preferences:** Temporary player names, selected language, sound effects toggle, and haptic feedback preferences.
- **Gameplay Progress:** Free round play counts, cooldown timestamps, and rewarded round credits.
- **Purchase Status Cache:** Local validation state verifying whether premium features have been unlocked.

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

---

### 4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)
If you choose to purchase an In-App Purchase (e.g., "Full App Unlock"):
- The transaction is processed directly and securely by **Apple Inc.** via Apple's StoreKit APIs.
- We do not have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.
- All purchase transactions are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
- The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs that track you across apps or websites.
- Any rewarded gameplay features operate using on-device timers and do not track or transmit personal identifiers.
- The App does not communicate with external analytics servers (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK).

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
King's Game complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because the App does not collect, retain, or transmit any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

---

### 7. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all data is stored exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage:
- You have complete control over your data.
- **Deleting the App:** If you uninstall or delete King's Game from your iOS device, all locally stored game data, preferences, and progress are immediately and permanently erased by iOS.

---

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

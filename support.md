## 📌 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I play King's Game?
King's Game is a pass-and-play party game designed for 2 or more players in the same room:
1. **Setup:** Add player names and pick your prompt packs or customize your own rules.
2. **The Draw:** Pass the phone around. Each player taps a card to draw their secret role. One player becomes the **King**, while everyone else receives a secret Subject number (#1, #2, etc.).
3. **The Royal Decree:** The King issues a command to specific numbers without knowing who holds which number (e.g., "Subject #1 and Subject #3 must do an impression of each other!").
4. **The Reveal:** Everyone reveals their numbers and the decree is fulfilled!

### 2. Can I play King's Game offline?
**Yes!** King's Game is 100% offline and designed for in-person group gatherings. No internet connection, cellular data, or Wi-Fi is required to play.

### 3. How do the Free Plays and Cooldowns work?
- All players receive free rounds to enjoy the game.
- Once free rounds are used, the counter replenishes after a cooldown period, or you can claim extra round credits via the in-app reward option.
- Purchasing the **Full App Unlock** grants permanent, unlimited rounds with zero cooldowns and no waiting.

### 4. How do I restore my purchase?
If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new iPhone or iPad:
1. Open King's Game.
2. Go to **Settings** (or tap the VIP banner on the home screen).
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. Sign in with the Apple ID used to make the original purchase. Your Full Unlock will restore immediately at no extra charge.

### 5. Why can't I hear sound effects or feel haptics?
- **Silent / Mute Switch:** Check the physical Ring/Silent switch on the side of your iPhone or the Action Button/Control Center mute state.
- **Volume Level:** Turn up your media volume using the side volume buttons.
- **In-App Settings:** Verify that sound effects and haptics are toggled **ON** in the King's Game Settings screen.
- **Low Power Mode:** iOS may reduce or disable haptic vibrations when Low Power Mode is active.

### 6. What iOS version is supported?
King's Game requires **iOS 17.0 or later** and is fully compatible with iPhone and iPad.
